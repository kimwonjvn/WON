# Privacy

WON does not collect, transmit, or sell any data. It has no telemetry, no analytics, no ads and no crash-report upload.

The app only makes network connections that you start yourself (FTP / SFTP / FTPS / SMB servers you add, and cloud-sync folders handled by their own provider apps).

WON is a fork of SPAN Finder; the upstream Sentry crash reporting was removed.
